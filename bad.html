Explanation of Glossing:
1P, 2P, 3P First, Second, Third person.
DIST, PROX Distal or proximal, “This one (here)” vs “That one (there)”
PST, PRES, FUT Past, Present, or Future tense
CONT Continuous. “X-ing”
DE Discontinued, something that was continuous but has since ended.
CAUS Causative, being made to be that way.
EXP Experiential (“I witnessed or felt this”)
INF Inferential (“I reckon”)
REP Reportative (“Some say that…”)
GKN General Knowledge (“It is known…”)
NP Noun Phrase marker
SUBR Subordinate Clause marker (“X, that/which Y, Z.”)
SAP Sapient, living, non-animal with the capacity for thought.
LAN Living animal
NLAN Nonliving animal
PLNT Living non-animal, plants, fungi, bacteria, etc.
NAT Nonliving natural thing, like rocks or water.
ART Nonliving, artiﬁcial. Buildings and such.
IMMA Immaterial, for discussing esoteric ball knowledge.
EXPL Explanatory (“Well…”)
ADV Adverb. Like adding “-ly” to the end of a word. “Quick” vs “Quick-ly”.
NEG Negative. (“ -n’t ”)
